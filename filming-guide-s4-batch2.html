Before You Start - Read Once

Camera

Resolution4K, 24 or 30fps
FormatPhone native (HEVC/MOV is fine)
StabilizationTripod or propped still, no handheld
OrientationVertical (9:16)
Film in landscape too if it's easy, but vertical is the one I actually need. Don't worry about upscaling or filters, I handle all of that in edit.

Framing

  • Chest-up, eyes about a third from the top of frame
  • Leave headroom, don't crop foreheads
  • For the duo video, sit close enough to share the frame, angled slightly toward each other
  • Clean background, no window directly behind you (backlight kills exposure)

Audio

  • Lav mic if you have one, phone mic is fine if the room is quiet
  • Record a 5 second test clip first and check it back before the real take
  • Close windows, turn off fans/HVAC in the room
  • For the duo video, keep both voices roughly equal distance from the mic

Delivery Notes

  • Film these back to back in one sitting if you can, it's the most efficient way to knock out 6
  • If you flub a line, just pause and restart that sentence, I'll cut around it
  • Slate each file name with the video title before sending
This batch becomes November's content bank, so it's worth getting done in one sitting rather than trickling in over two weeks.
Closing Disclaimer - Use On Every Video

Every video needs a verbal or on-screen close along these lines before you stop recording. This is the same line we run in the first comment on posts, so it should feel familiar.

Standard close

"Not advice, just perspective. Your situation is unique, let's talk about it."

If compliance has a different approved verbal line for on-camera use (vs. the comment version), send it over and I'll swap it into this guide.

Video 01
CPA vs. Financial Planning
Duo - Nichole + Jalyn Must film ~75 sec
Gear
  • One phone, both of you in frame
Before you hit record
  • Both of you on camera, decide who opens and build off each other
  • End on the open question, not "book a call"
Open with this - knowing, not preachy
"My CPA handles my taxes, so why do I need financial planning?"
Outline (talk it through together, not word for word)
Point 1
CPA vs. advisor are different roles, tax prep vs. proactive planning.
Point 2
Decisions that should happen before tax season, not during it.
Close
"Tax planning shouldn't start when your return is being prepared." Leave it there.
Close with
"Not advice, just perspective. Your situation is unique, let's talk about it."
