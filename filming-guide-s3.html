Filming Guide

6 Videos  ·  2 Buckets  ·  1 Weekly Standing Segment  ·  Duo + Solo Mix

Before You Start - Read Once

Camera (iPhone 16 Pro)

Resolution4K, 30fps
FormatProRes off, standard
StabilizationAction mode ON
OrientationVertical (9:16)
Same setup as Session 1 and 2. If anything moved, re-check exposure and reset the framing guide.

Framing

  • Chest-up framing, same as prior sessions
  • Eyeline just above the lens, not down at the screen
  • Leave headroom, don't crop the top of the frame tight
  • Duo shots: both people visible, don't let one lean out of frame

Audio

  • Lav mic connected and tested before first take
  • Close windows/doors, check for HVAC hum

Delivery Notes

  • Confident, not rushed - let the hook land before continuing
  • These are technical topics - slow down slightly on numbers so they're easy to caption/subtitle accurately
  • Re-take if a line feels stiff, don't force it
Closing Disclaimer - Pick One Per Video

Every video needs a compliance disclaimer read at the end. Pick whichever option fits the pacing of the video naturally.

Option 1 (Recommended)

"Not advice, just perspective - your situation is unique, let's talk about it."

Option 2

"This is our take, not personalized advice - reach out if you want to dig into your specific situation."

Option 3

"Everything we share is our opinion - for advice specific to you, let's have a real conversation."

Say it plainly, not rushed. It should sound like a natural close, not a legal read. These topics are technical - resist the urge to over-explain past the script, the disclaimer covers the rest.
